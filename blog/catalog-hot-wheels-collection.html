How to Catalog a Hot Wheels Collection: 5 Methods, Ranked

From memory to multi-brand database — ranked worst to best.

Every collector hits the same wall: you can't remember what you own, you buy duplicates, and your "inventory" is a camera roll of blurry shelf photos. Here are the five ways collectors catalog, ranked worst to best.

5. Memory

Free. Also fictional past about 300 cars. You WILL buy your third identical Super Treasure Hunt.

4. Spreadsheets

Works, until it doesn't. No photos, no series data, and entering 2,000 cars by hand is a weekend you'll never get back. Exporting is the one thing spreadsheets do well — keep that in mind.

3. Photo albums

Fast to capture, impossible to search. "Which mainline series was the blue '67 Custom Mustang from?" Good luck scrolling 4,000 photos.

2. Single-brand apps

Fine if you collect exactly one brand. Most of us don't — Hot Wheels, Matchbox, M2, GreenLight, Mini GT, Tomica, and Kaido House collectors overlap heavily.

1. A multi-brand database with photos
